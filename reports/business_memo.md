# Choosing comparable Airbnb listings in Toronto

**To Toronto hosts and property managers**  
**From Lambert Tan**

Start with listings that match the property’s room type, guest capacity and bathroom arrangement. In the November 2025 Toronto sample, the median asking price was CAD 167 for an entire home and CAD 65 for a private room. A neighbourhood median that combines the two can give a misleading benchmark.

Across 15,332 listings, the fitted model associated an entire home with a **43.8% higher** nightly price than a private room after accounting for the other measured characteristics. A shared bathroom was associated with a **21.0% lower** price. For otherwise similar listings, each additional kilometre from Union Station was associated with a **2.7% lower** nightly price. [Model estimates](../outputs/model_results/model_effects.csv)

For a two-bedroom apartment, I would first compare nearby entire homes with similar guest capacity and bathroom facilities. If the comparison area needs to be widened, distance from Union Station provides a consistent location measure. The [Tableau dashboard](../dashboard/README.md) shows how median asking prices vary across distance bands.

These figures describe one month’s advertised rates. The dataset does not show what guests actually paid, how often a listing was booked, or whether a different price would increase revenue.
